# Design direction

## Field register

The app uses the visual grammar of a well-made field register: strong Devanagari, ruled separators, stamped status language, and a literal ladder that turns administrative progress into something a worker can scan. It is calm and dignified rather than rustic or decorative.

- Exact palette: soil brown `#6B4226`, wheat `#F4E3B2`, leaf green `#4C7A3F`, sun amber `#E8912D`, terracotta red `#B5482C`, ink `#2B2420`.
- Hindi is the default; English is an explicit switch, never a second page.
- Tiro Devanagari Hindi for display copy, Noto Sans Devanagari for Hindi body copy, Fraunces for English display, and Inter for English body copy, all with generous line-height and strong weight steps.
- Soft 12–15px corners for task surfaces; full pills only for compact language/status controls.
- One expressive moment: the current ladder rung reveals on mount. Everything else is instant or tactile press feedback.
- No decorative imagery, generic gradients, admin dashboards, or fake government precision.
